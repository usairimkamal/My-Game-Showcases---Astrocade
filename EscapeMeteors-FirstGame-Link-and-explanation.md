## 🎮 About the Game

Escape Meteors is a survival dodging game — meteors of different types rain down across a variety of maps, and it's up to you to stay alive as long as possible. The longer you survive, the harder it gets... and the better your rewards.

---

## ⭐ Fever Mode

Collect **Star Dust Coins** scattered across the map to fill up your **Fever Bar**. Once it's full, Fever Mode kicks in — making you **immortal** for a short time, letting you push through waves you'd never survive otherwise.

---

## 🤖 Mini Robot Companion

A friendly little robot fights by your side, automatically **absorbing meteors** and converting them into Star Dust for you. It helps you:

- 🎨 Unlock new **skins** faster
- ⚡ Trigger **Fever Mode** earlier

## 🔥 Difficulty Levels

Choose your challenge — from a relaxed dodge to a true test of survival.

| Difficulty | Vibe | Protective Wall Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Easy** | Chill and beginner-friendly | ~40 seconds |
| 🔵 **Normal** | Balanced challenge | ~1 min 30 sec+ |
| 🟠 **Hard** | Intense and unforgiving | 2 min – 10 min |
| ⚫ **Impossible** | Truly brutal — built for the fearless | ~1 hour |

> 🟡 *Medium Difficulty is being considered for a future update.*

### 🧱 About Protection Walls
Walls can shield you from meteors — but once they break, they need time to come back. The cooldown scales dramatically with difficulty, from a quick 40-second respawn on Easy all the way up to a full hour on Impossible. Use them wisely!
